# Key Business Insights – Olist Ecommerce Intelligence Project

## Executive Insights

* Total ecommerce revenue exceeded ₹16M.
* More than 99K orders were analyzed.
* Monthly revenue trend showed strong business growth over time.
* Repeat customer percentage remained relatively low, indicating customer retention opportunities.

---

# Customer Insights

## Regional Performance

* SP generated the highest overall ecommerce revenue.
* Different states showed significant variation in Average Order Value (AOV).

## Payment Behavior

* Credit card was the dominant payment method across the platform.
* Voucher and debit card usage remained relatively low.

## Customer Activity

* Monthly order trends showed increasing customer engagement over time.
* Customer purchasing behavior varied across regions and states.

---

# Product & Sales Insights

## Revenue Analysis

* Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts were the top revenue-generating categories.
* Some product categories generated high revenue despite moderate order volumes.

## Product Popularity

* Bed Bath Table category had the highest order volume.
* Furniture & Decor and Sports & Leisure also showed strong customer demand.

## Pricing Analysis

* Most products belonged to affordable price ranges.
* Premium-priced products existed but represented a smaller portion of total products.

## Logistics Analysis

* Freight cost varied significantly across categories.
* Larger product categories showed higher average shipping costs.

---

# Delivery & Operational Insights

## Order Fulfillment

* More than 97% of orders were successfully delivered.
* Canceled and unavailable orders represented a very small percentage.

## Delivery Performance

* On-time delivery percentage remained very strong.
* Average delivery duration was approximately 12 days.
* Late delivery percentage remained relatively low.

## Operational Trends

* Monthly delivery trends showed increasing operational workload over time.
* Logistics performance remained stable despite increasing order volume.

---

# Business Impact

This project helped identify:

* high-performing product categories
* customer purchasing behavior
* operational delivery efficiency
* pricing distribution trends
* regional revenue performance
* customer retention opportunities
